YouTube Downloader

Download authorized videos as MP4 or extract audio as MP3.

Backend connection

For use on this PC, keep http://localhost:5000 and run the supplied start-downloader.bat file. For a public service, enter the HTTPS address of your deployed downloader server.

Only download content you are legally entitled to download. This tool does not bypass DRM, private-video access controls, or other restrictions. YouTube's terms may restrict downloading.
