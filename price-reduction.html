Drag-to-Remove PDF Tool

Choose the removal tool once, then drag over any price or text. Each area is removed as soon as you release.

Page —

Drag mode is OFF. Turn it on once, then drag across any text/price. Each drag is applied instantly.

How it works

  • Enable drag-to-remove once, then drag over as many areas as you want. Every completed drag is applied immediately.
  • Works with selectable-text and scanned/image PDFs because you mark visible areas.
  • Export creates a flattened PDF copy. The original is unchanged.
Please check the page before exporting. Rectangle removal covers everything inside the box, so keep the box tight around the text you want removed. The exported PDF is image-based; text search/copy may be lost.
