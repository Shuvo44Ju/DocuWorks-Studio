1. General Information
DescriptionCell counter system designed for rapid and accurate determination of cell concentration, viability, and morphology using image-based analysis technology. The system supports analysis of suspension and adherent cell cultures with minimal sample volume requirement and provides automated data acquisition, result analysis, graphical representation, data storage, and export functionalities suitable for research and laboratory applications.
Sample Analysis CapacityCapable of analyzing viable and total cell concentration, viability, and cell morphology for multiple samples.
